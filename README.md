# The Book of Chris T.

An interactive, book-style portfolio site for **Chris Trotter** — presented as a paper-bound book you can flip through, page by page.

🔗 Live at [cmtrotter.com](https://cmtrotter.com)

## What it is

Rather than a traditional scrolling résumé, the site renders Chris's career as a turnable book. Visitors flip through chapters — an "About" page, case-study spreads for Google, Capital One, and JCPenney, plus sections on AI competencies, tools, and education. Two companion pages ([capital-one.html](capital-one.html) and [google.html](google.html)) hold deeper case-study detail linked from the book.

## How it's built

- **Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** — no framework or build step.
- **[StPageFlip](https://github.com/Nodlik/StPageFlip)** (the `page-flip` library, loaded via CDN) powers the page-turning animation, with a single-page mode on mobile.
- **Crimson Text** (Google Fonts) for the serif, printed-book feel, plus an SVG fractal-noise overlay for paper grain and layered shadows for depth.
- **GitHub Pages** hosting with a custom domain set via the `CNAME` file (`cmtrotter.com`).

## Who Chris is

Chris Trotter is a **Senior UX Content Strategist** with 10+ years of experience leading content design for global products in highly regulated industries. He spent 2017–2026 at **Google**, where he was the sole and lead content designer on the 2022 Google Wallet re-launch and shaped content strategy across GPay, GPay India, and Payments with Google Assistant. Earlier roles include **Capital One** (financial UX writing) and **JCPenney** (where he created "Penny," an AI assistant persona, and authored the enterprise style guide).

His focus today is on generative-AI content strategy, trust and ethics in AI, and information architecture — translating complex technical and legal requirements into clear, human-centered experiences. He now offers specialized 1099 consultancy for high-growth firms and enterprise teams, and is based in San Francisco, where he lives with his golden retriever, Champ.
